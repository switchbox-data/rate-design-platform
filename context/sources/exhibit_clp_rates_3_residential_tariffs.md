# Exhibit CLP-RATES-3 — Clean and Scored Tariffs (Residential schedules only: Rate 1, 5, 6, 7)

**Source**: `Exhibit CLP-RATES-3.pdf`
**Pages extracted**: 243–258 of 439 (residential schedules only, from Schedule E-1.1)
**Docket**: Connecticut PURA Docket No. 26-05-10 — Application of The Connecticut Light and Power Company d/b/a Eversource Energy to Amend Its Rate Schedules
**Exhibit**: CLP-RATES-3, **Schedule E-1.1** ("Unmarked Copy of Proposed Tariffs" — clean, final proposed tariff language, effective July 1, 2027)

**Extraction method**: `pdftotext -layout`. This is a **scoped extract**: the full 439-page exhibit contains the entire tariff book — General Terms & Conditions for Delivery Service and Generation Supply (~pages 3–240), and every rate schedule (Rate 1 through Rate 119, EV classes, street lighting) in two forms: **Schedule E-1.0** (a redlined "scored" copy showing changes from the current tariff) and **Schedule E-1.1** (the clean "unmarked" copy of the final proposed tariff). Only the residential rate schedules (Rate 1, 5, 6, 7) from the clean **Schedule E-1.1** copy were transcribed here. The redlined **Schedule E-1.0** copy of these same schedules (pages ~243–259 of the E-1.0 section, earlier in the document) was **not** transcribed: `pdftotext` extraction of that section interleaves struck-through (old) and inserted (new) text into a single unreadable string (e.g. "JanuaryMay 20158" for a redline changing "January 1, 2015" to "May 1, 2018"), and reconstructing it reliably would require page-by-page visual inspection. If the _current_ (pre-case) tariff language or rates are needed, either use the redlined pages via visual inspection, or note that the "Current Rate" columns in `exhibit_clp_rates_2_residential.md` (from `Exhibit CLP-RATES-2.4`) already give the current per-unit rates for Rate 1, 5, and 7 without needing the redlined tariff text.

**Data-quality note**: In the source PDF, the Rate 6 schedule (pages 249–251) is immediately followed by a near-identical duplicate (pages 252–254) — same customer charge, same block rates, same all charges, with only a minor difference in the schedule title line ("OPTIONAL RESIDENTIAL ELECTRIC SPACE HEATING SERVICE" vs. "OPTIONAL RESIDENTIAL SPACE HEATING SERVICE"). This duplication is in the source PDF itself, not an artifact of this extraction. Only one copy is transcribed below.

All schedules carry the same "Supersedes [Rate]" footer showing the current tariff being replaced (effective May 1, 2026, by Motion Ruling No. 10 dated April 29, 2026, Docket No. 26-01-03) is superseded by the proposed tariff (effective July 1, 2027, Docket No. 26-05-10); this is omitted below except where noted.

## Rate 5 — Residential Electric Heating Service (pages 246–248)

**Available for**: the entire electrical requirements of single-family residences, residential outbuildings, individual apartments, and general service use (common areas) in apartment buildings, where residential use constitutes over 50 percent of metered energy (same apartment/farm/veterans/campground/marina provisions as Rate 1, cross-referencing the same Conn.
