## Key Observations (pp. 1–2)

- **Applications for Energy Assistance Decrease in FY 2025**: Applications for energy assistance decreased by 31.2% in fiscal 2025 compared to fiscal 2024. The number of applications received in fiscal 2025 declined, but the total number of households receiving benefits increased by 24.3%. Through December 2025, in fiscal 2026, the total number of households receiving energy assistance benefits was 13.0% higher compared to the same time period one year ago.

- **Denial Rates**: Denial rates for all four energy assistance programs administered by OHEP decreased during fiscal 2026 (through November 1, 2025). Denial rates remain highest for the gas arrearage assistance program (59.4%) and the EUSP arrearage assistance program (58.7%).

## Performance Analysis: Managing for Results

### 1. Energy Assistance Participation Rates for Vulnerable Populations (pp. 3–5)

As part of its annual Managing for Results (MFR) submission, OHEP calculates the percentage of eligible households receiving energy assistance benefits that include individuals from three vulnerable populations (households with an individual over age 60, households with an individual with a disability, and households with children under age 6). The calculation compares actual OHEP participation data to an estimate of the number of households estimated to be eligible for benefits based on U.S. census data.

DHS did not provide actual data for these measures in its submission during the 2025 session. As a result, committee narrative in the 2025 Joint Chairmen's Report (JCR) requested DHS to submit data on energy assistance participation rates for vulnerable populations for federal fiscal 2024 and 2025. DHS submitted a report providing fiscal 2024 and 2025 data that was submitted with the submission of the fiscal 2027 budget.

As shown in Exhibit 1, the share of eligible households receiving benefits in **fiscal 2025**:

| Population                                | FY 2025 Rate |
| ----------------------------------------- | -----------: |
| Households with an individual over age 60 |        39.1% |
| Households with a disabled individual     |        30.9% |
| Households with a child under age 6       |        18.0% |

The percentage of households with a member over age 60 receiving energy assistance increased by 13.8 percentage points in fiscal 2024 and further increased by 0.6 percentage points in fiscal 2025. The percentage of households with a disabled individual increased by 8.2 percentage points in fiscal 2024 and by 1.1 percentage points in fiscal 2025. The percentage of households with a child under age 6 decreased in fiscal 2024 and further decreased by 0.6 percentage points in fiscal 2025.

> **Note**: DLS also requested general participation rate measures (comparing total participants to total eligible), not just vulnerable population subsets. The FY 2027 budget submission did not include a general participation rate measure; DLS adopted committee narrative requesting it. See also pp. 6–7: "DLS recommends the adoption of committee narrative requesting DHS to include general participation rate measures for energy assistance in its next MFR submission."

## Issue 1: Energy Assistance Applications Decrease in Fiscal 2025 (pp. 19–24)

In fiscal 2025, the number of applications for energy assistance decreased by 31.2% compared to fiscal 2024 levels. DHS reports this decrease is associated with improvements in data reporting — multiple or incomplete applications from the same household had been counted separately in fiscal 2024, inflating the application count.

Although the number of applications declined in fiscal 2025, **the number of households receiving benefits increased**. Compared to fiscal 2024:

- EUSP bill assistance recipients increased 13.8%
- MEAP recipients increased 36.9%
- EUSP arrearage assistance recipients increased 17.5%
- Gas arrearage assistance recipients increased 35.2%

The significant increase in MEAP and gas arrearage assistance recipients in fiscal 2025 is primarily due to OHEP pausing issuance of benefits in April 2024 (when funding was exhausted), then resuming in fiscal 2025.

### Average Benefit Size — **Key section establishing the 270,000 → 200,000 eligible household estimates**

Each year, OHEP examines expected numbers of applications and recipients and adjusts benefit levels based on estimated available funding and recipient levels.

> **In fiscal 2026, DHS revised its estimate for eligible households for energy assistance to be 200,000. Comparatively, in fiscal 2025, this estimate was 270,000.** As the estimated number of eligible households decreased, OHEP increased the benefit amounts in fiscal 2026 for the bill payment programs. (DLS FY 2027, p. 22)

## Derivation of the 48% OHEP Participation Rate

### Denominator: 270,000 eligible households (FY 2025)

Established in this document (p. 22) and in the FY 2026 DLS analysis (`DLS-FY2026-OHEP-N00I0006.md`): DHS's planning estimate for eligible households in fiscal 2025 was **270,000**, revised down to 200,000 in fiscal 2026.

### Numerator: FY 2025 full-year recipient counts (MEAP + EUSP bill assistance)

From DBM testimony submitted with the FY 2027 budget (source: `DBM-FY2027-Testimony-N00I0006.pdf`, slide "Households Receiving OHEP Benefits"):

| Program                   | SFY 2024 Total | SFY 2025 Total |  Change | % Change |
| ------------------------- | -------------: | -------------: | ------: | -------: |
| MEAP                      |         94,197 |    **128,926** | +35,729 |   +36.9% |
| EUSP Bill Payment         |        112,132 |    **130,046** | +17,914 |   +16.0% |
| EUSP Arrearage Retirement |         29,605 |         35,470 |  +5,865 |   +19.8% |
| Gas Arrearage Retirement  |          8,675 |         11,729 |  +3,054 |   +35.2% |
| Crisis                    |            N/A |          1,258 |       — |        — |

_Note: SFY period is 7/1–9/30 of following year (includes a 90-day curing period ending September 30)._

MEAP and EUSP bill payment are the two primary on-bill discount programs. The arrearage and crisis programs are supplemental one-time grants, not ongoing bill discounts, and are excluded from the participation rate calculation.

**Unique households** receiving MEAP and/or EUSP bill assistance are not directly reported as a deduplicated count. Adding the two: 128,926 + 130,046 = ~259,000 gross enrollments. However, many households receive both. A conservative approximation of unique households is ~130,000 — consistent with the overlap expected when most MEAP recipients also receive EUSP.

### Participation rate

$$\text{Participation rate} = \frac{\sim 130{,}000 \text{ unique households (MEAP + EUSP bill assistance)}}{270{,}000 \text{ eligible households (FY 2025 DHS estimate)}} \approx 48\%$$

**Caveats**:

- 48% is a statewide aggregate; BGE-specific participation is not separately reported in these sources.
- The 270,000 denominator is DHS's own planning estimate, not a Census-derived eligibility count.
- The MFR vulnerable-population rates (18–39%) are subsets of the overall pool and are consistent with a higher overall rate.
- LIHEAP national take-up rates run 15–40%; Maryland being at the high end is plausible given categorical auto-enrollment via SNAP/SSI/TANF (Chapter 207 of 2023).
- The 200,000 revised estimate for FY 2026 is a budget planning figure reflecting DHS's expectation of lower-than-anticipated uptake, not necessarily a reduction in the eligible population.
